JWST captures a first look at a young exoplanet's atmosphere

Eric Berger · 4 min read

The James Webb Space Telescope's near-infrared spectrograph has detected water vapor, methane, and an unexpectedly thick layer of silicate clouds wrapped around the gas giant.

"It's the first time we've directly probed weather on a world this young."
